Week by week

Six weeks. Every week you build something real.

Every week has the same rhythm: short pre-reads, one live session with Sid, and something real you build before the next one.

01WEEK

The AI PM Role

How AI products behave differently from normal software, and what that changes about your job. The questions to ask at every stage. The four common ways AI projects fail. How to prioritise an AI roadmap.

You build: Second Brain v0, running on your machine.
02WEEK

How AI actually works

How an LLM produces an answer, and why it sometimes makes one up. How RAG lets AI answer from your own data. Prompts that get reliable results. What embeddings do, and what an agent can and cannot do.

You build: RAG into your Second Brain — you learn it by building it.
03WEEK

Product sense, design and evals

How to take apart any AI product. The AI-First PRD. How to define what "good" means, build a golden set, and use one model to grade another. Guest session on running evals in production.

You build: a real eval suite for your own product.
04WEEK

Build with Claude Code

The build loop that stops AI projects spiralling. The agent architecture decision tree: one call, a pipeline, sub-agents or multi-agent. MCP and connectors. Keeping running costs sensible.

You build: a multi-agent application, live, together.
05WEEK

Testing and launch

The testing loop: test, judge, diagnose, fix, retest. Must-pass, edge and fail-safe cases. The four launch audits — security, code quality, prompt injection, cost and latency. Deploying to a live URL.

You build: your own product, tested and live.
06WEEK

Demo Day

You present your live product to the cohort and get structured feedback from peers and from Sid. Plus positioning your AI product, writing a launch post, and adding payments if you want to charge for it.

You leave with: a demo video and a portfolio one-pager.

Questions people ask

Do I need to code?

No. You direct Claude Code, which writes it. Being comfortable reading code helps, but past students who had never written a line shipped working products.

Do I need an idea first?

Helpful, not required. You pressure-test ideas in week two and write the full spec in week three, by which point you have already built two products.

Who is this not for?

People wanting a pure ML engineering course, anyone brand new to product management, and anyone who can't commit 4–6 hours a week.

Are sessions recorded?

Yes, shared within 24 hours, and yours for life. Live is still where the real value is.

Can my employer pay?

Around 90% of Cohort 1 students were reimbursed through their company's learning budget. We provide the documentation to support the request.

What if I miss a week?

Watch the recording and ask in the cohort Slack. Sid answers there between sessions.
