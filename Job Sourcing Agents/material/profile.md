# Profile

The sourcer reads this to rank roles. A CV says what you have done; this says what you want next, what you would refuse, and what you would trade away. Write plain sentences, two or three lines each. Be honest — nobody sees this but you, and a polished version produces a polished ranking that is wrong.

Replace each prompt below with your real answer.

## What roles are you actually going for?
> Describe the job, not the label — titles vary wildly between companies. "Owning a product area end to end, with engineers reporting into the work" beats "Senior PM".

## Where can you work?
> City, country, remote, hybrid. Say which you would move for and which you would not.

## Do you need visa sponsorship anywhere?
> Be direct. It changes which roles are worth ranking at all.

## Company size and stage
> Early startup, scale-up, large company. What have you enjoyed, what would you avoid.

## Industries you want, industries you would rule out
> Include the ones you would refuse on principle — it saves the agent wasting slots.

## Money
> A minimum you would not go below, and a number you are aiming at. Currency included.

## What you want more of in the next role
> More scope, more technical depth, more people management, more autonomy, better pay, less travel.

## What you are done with
> The thing you will not do again. Often the most useful line in the file.

## Anything unusual about your situation
> Career break, career change, relocating, first role after a founder stint. Say it plainly — the sourcer ranks differently when it knows.
