## The differentiator (Epic 5.1 — LOCKED)

**Most "AI courses" teach you to *chat* with a model. BWCC teaches you to *direct* one.**

You don't learn *about* AI — you walk out with **three real, deployed products on live URLs that you built in four weeks, without writing code.** It removes "I'm not technical enough" from the conversation.

Why it's *positioning*, not a feature: competitors sell prompts, theory, or "AI literacy." BWCC sells **shipped output + the operating loop** (direct → read → push back → iterate) plus a reusable `CLAUDE.md`. The proof is the URL, not the certificate.
