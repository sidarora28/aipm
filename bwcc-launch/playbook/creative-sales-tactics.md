# Creative sales tactics — keep on file

Brainstormed 2026-06-20 during scoping session. Not yet deployed. Pulled out so they don't get lost as MASTERPLAN evolves.

## The 7 ideas worth keeping

### 1. Public buyer list, live-updated
Add a section to the checkout page (or repeated in LinkedIn posts) that shows actual names + LinkedIn URLs of every BWCC 003 buyer so far, updated in real-time. "Sid, Carla, Afra, Ajit, Sandeep, Philleacock — and you?" Names + faces beats "47 seats taken" as a scarcity mechanism — PMs see other PMs they recognise, want in.

### 2. The objection contest
Email blast to engaged list: "Reply with your single biggest reason for NOT joining BWCC. The 5 best objections I get, I'll publicly address in a video this Friday. Everyone who replies gets early-bird extended 48 hours." Pays prospects to tell you why they're not buying. Surfaces real friction + nudges fence-sitters into a conversation. The video becomes a long-tail sales asset.

### 3. The 10th-buyer stunt
Single LinkedIn post: "Whoever is the 10th BWCC buyer this week — I'm Venmoing them their $450 back." That's it. The post is the campaign. Costs $450. The bet generates 30-50 buyers chasing the lottery — because everyone thinks they could be the 10th. The 11th-50th still bought.

### 4. Co-sell with one specific alumni
Pick one credible AIPMA alum. They host a 30-minute open call: "I'll tell you what BWCC will actually do for you, from someone who already paid for Sid's stuff." Send them 50 prospects. They convert at 3-5x because they're a peer. Cut: $200 per buyer. Cheap and unusual — alumni don't usually become sellers.

### 5. Midnight close on LinkedIn Live
One night during launch week, go live at 11pm UK / 6pm ET / 3pm PT. Public open Q&A. At midnight sharp, close early-bird in real time, on stream. Comments-to-buy: people quote-tweet "I'm in," you DM the checkout link. Theater of the close. Most PMs have never bought a course at a specific moment with the founder watching — beats every countdown timer.

### 6. Group of 3, all-or-none
"Get 2 PMs to join with you, all 3 lock in tonight, all 3 pay $300 each instead of $450." Trigger is all 3, simultaneously. Flips the buying dynamic from solo evaluation to peer-pressure threesome. One PM convinces two friends — that's a different conversation than "should I buy this course."

### 7. The personalized 60-second Loom
DM/email your top 50 most-engaged non-buyers: "Send me your role + one problem in your day-to-day. I'll record a 60-second Loom showing you literally what you'd build in BWCC for THAT specific problem." Then do it. Half won't reply. Half of those who do will buy after the Loom. ~25 Looms = ~10-15 conversions = $5-7K from one afternoon.

## Where these fit into the live plan

- **Cart-abandon flow for BWCC clickers (Email epic, task 1.5)** — strong fit for #2 (objection contest) and #7 (personalized Loom). Cart abandoners are high-intent, named, and one nudge away.
- **Twitter 2x/day series (Social epic, task 4.2)** — natural home for #3 (10th-buyer stunt) and #5 (midnight close).
- **Pre-cohort events (Events epic)** — #4 (alumni co-sell call) could be one of the 4-weekend events.
- **Retargeting ad creative (Ads epic)** — #1 (public buyer list as social proof) could be the visual hook.

## Top bets if forced to pick

- **#3 (10th-buyer stunt)** for top-of-funnel pulse, single-day campaign.
- **#7 (personalized Looms)** for bottom-of-funnel conversion, week-long effort.
- **#2 (objection contest)** for surfacing the real reasons people aren't buying, useful as intelligence even if it doesn't close anyone directly.
