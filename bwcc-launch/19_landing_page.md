## The four weeks

**Week 1 — Setup + workflow**
Get Claude Code installed on your machine pointing at a real codebase. Learn the difference between using it as a chatbot and using it as a junior engineer. Build your first prompt patterns.

**Week 2 — PRD to prototype**
Bring a feature you speced this quarter. We turn the PRD into a working prototype together, live. By the end of the week you have something runnable.

**Week 3 — Ship it to a user**
Get the prototype in front of one real user. Capture what you learn. Iterate. This is the part most PMs never get to do.

**Week 4 — Make it stick**
Scale the workflow into your week-to-week PM work. Research, synthesis, roadmap drafts, comp teardowns. You leave with a process, not a one-time win.
