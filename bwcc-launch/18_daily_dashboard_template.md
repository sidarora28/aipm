## Decision rules
- **If Day 3 (Jun 22) < 4 cumulative sales**: the launch email isn't converting. Rewrite subject + first line, resend to non-openers.
- **If Day 7 (Jun 26) < 10 cumulative**: kill the channel doing worst, double-down on the channel doing best. Move budget/attention.
- **If Day 14 (Jul 3) < 30 cumulative**: stop adding new content. Switch entirely to direct outreach (DMs, personal emails) for the final 9 days.
- **If by Jul 8, < 50 cumulative**: hard pivot. Open a one-time payment plan (3 × $150) for the final 4 days and email the list about it.

## Channel kill-rules
- Email open rate < 30% on email 1 → rewrite emails 2+
- Tweet impressions < 2K average across launch week → reduce frequency, increase quality
- LinkedIn impressions < 1K → switch to direct DM outreach to LI connections
