## events lane · drafted 2026-06-22 · a guide for Sid to write from

**Goal:** maximise **show-up rate** — the single most sensitive lever in the funnel (`conversion-playbook.md`). A registrant who forgets or de-prioritises = a lost cohort sale. A daily touch keeps the event top-of-mind, builds anticipation, and runs the referral loop.

**⚠️ Confirm before sending — date/day:** 28 Jun 2026 is a *Sunday*; the live page says "*Saturday* 28 June." Lock the real event day/date — every email hard-codes it.

**Channel note:** this is the EMAIL series. Reminders *also* run via the WhatsApp group (per the registration flow). Keep them complementary, not word-for-word duplicates. *(Confirm signups capture email at registration — Sid.)*

**Event facts (appear in every email):**
- [Day] 28 Jun · 12:00 London / 7:00 ET / 4:30 IST · 90 min + live Q&A
- FREE · LIVE ONLY · **NO REPLAY**
- Watch a real product (an Email Assistant) built end-to-end; leave with the starter kits, dropped live.

## The 4 beats every email repeats (the drumbeat)

1. **When + add-to-calendar** — time in their timezone, one-tap calendar link.
2. **NO REPLAY** — "there's no recording; what you miss, you miss." Fresh phrasing each day.
3. **Larger-than-life** — this isn't a webinar; it's the day "I'm not technical enough" stops being true. Aspirational, big.
4. **Referral CTA** — "bring someone who needs to see this" + their share link (soft P.S., not the main ask).

## Referral mechanic (PROPOSE — needs your lock)

- **How:** each registrant gets a unique share link (or "forward this + have them drop your name"). Track referrals.
- **Reward — pick one:**
  1. **Refer 2 who register → unlock a bonus template/kit** (cheap, digital, on-brand).
  2. **Refer 3 → a seat in a live group hot-seat / 1:1 with Sid** (scarce, high-value).
  3. **Leaderboard → top referrer wins [a free BWCC seat / a 1:1]** (most viral, biggest prize).
- **Framing:** always "help a friend level up," never "spam your network." Rides the build-in-public energy.
- *This also unblocks the parked Beehiiv referral automation.*

## Tone & rules

- Excited, warm, human — flowing sentences, **not** choppy Twitter lines (house style). 90–150 words.
- **One primary CTA per email** (show up / add to calendar); referral is the soft P.S.
- **Don't teach the build** — tease value only, so the live-only kits keep their pull.
- **Don't hard-sell the cohort here** — this series is about *attendance*. The cohort sell happens in-event + post-event (`conversion-playbook.md`).
