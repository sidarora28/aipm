## The build (for context — full spec lives in 02-co-build.md, to be rewritten)

A 5-agent system for social content creation, built live end-to-end:

1. **Viral Finder** — pulls the top-performing reels in the niche
2. **Ranker** — scores on real signals, picks the top 7
3. **Angle Generator** — creates multiple angles for each of the 7
4. **Script Studio** — 3 sub-agents (hook writer, copywriter, fact-checker) learn from what works, produce 20 scripts per angle
5. **The Analyst** — studies Sid's own posted reels, learns what wins and why, feeds it back to the writer

Mapped to 3 milestones: **Research engine** (1+2) · **Writing engine** (3+4) · **Learning engine** (5).

## Why this page converts where W1 didn't

Event 1: 95 of 250 showed, 0 bought — the pitch was the problem, and the W1 page had no cohort pre-frame and no proof the *attendee* could do this.

Two upgrades baked in here:

1. **Meta-proof, woven through.** W1 said "watch me build a thing." W2 says "watch me build *the thing that made the content you already follow me for.*" The host stats stop being a bio and become the live demo — 75K LinkedIn is the machine's output. Proof, not promise. This is the biggest lever the flat W1 page lacked.
2. **A light cohort pre-frame** low on the page (see "after the 90 minutes"). Pre-disclosing the pitch disarms it on the day and warms registrants toward the cohort before they show up — so the live pitch isn't a cold ask to a room that only wanted free kits.

The page's job is still **free registrations**. The cohort is pre-framed, not hard-sold here — the pitch happens live.
