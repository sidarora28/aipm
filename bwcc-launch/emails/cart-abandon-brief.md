# Brief — BWCC Cart-Abandon Email Flow
## For the email-writing session · drafted 2026-06-22 · Epic 1.5

**Segment:** people who clicked the BWCC checkout (https://buildwithcc.vercel.app/paid) and didn't buy. High-intent.
**Exclude:** AIPMA alumni (separate $300 automation — do **not** include).
**Goal:** convert the click into a purchase. Single CTA every email: **buy** → https://buildwithcc.vercel.app/paid
**Pricing context:** $450 / $600. **Do not lead with a discount** — the price is fair; sell value first.

## Flow shape — 3 emails
1. **Email 1 — the soft nudge (~1–2h after abandon).** "You were one click away." Acknowledge they looked, remove the friction, restate the single most compelling outcome — *you'll have shipped something real by the end of week 1.* CTA: lock your seat.
2. **Email 2 — objection + proof (next day).** Address the most common silent objection (time / "can I actually do this" / fit). One concrete proof point or alum result. Reassure on the no-engineering-needed angle. CTA: buy.
3. **Email 3 — scarcity close (day 3).** Early-bird closing / seats filling / price jumps. "I don't want you coming back next week to find the seat's gone." CTA: buy.

## House style (from CLAUDE.md — bake these in)
- Write for humans — flowing full sentences, **not** choppy three-word lines.
- **One CTA only: buy.** No "book a call AND sign up AND reply."
- Scarcity for non-price-objectors: early-bird-closing + seats-filling.
- Keep each email **80–120 words** — readable on a phone in 20 seconds.
- Don't re-pitch the whole curriculum; reinforce the single outcome.
