Most people I know who care about AI have never shipped an AI product.

Not because they can't.

Because nobody ever showed them the loop.

🧵

1/ The loop is small.

Claude Code writes the code. You direct it.
- Pick the tool
- Pick the model
- Read what it produced
- Push back when it's wrong
- Iterate until it ships

That's the entire skill.

2/ The CLAUDE.md file is the unlock.

A small markdown file Claude reads every session. Tells Claude who you are, what this project is, how you like to work.

Drop one in any repo and Claude operates at your level. It's the single highest-leverage thing in the course.

3/ Three Claudes > one Claude.

A multi-agent product is just a lead Claude coordinating specialist Claudes. Anthropic shipped first-party orchestration in May 2026, built into Claude Code.

In the cohort you build the AI Interview Coach — three Claudes (interviewer, grader, coach) working together.

4/ Most AI products are over-architected.

Single Claude call does ~80% of products well. Don't be embarrassed by that.

The three-question test:
- Can one call do this?
- Is the work parallelisable?
- Does each step need its own context?

Only if no/yes/yes do you reach for sub-agents.
