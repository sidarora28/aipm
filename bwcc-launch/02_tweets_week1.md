## Launch day thread (Jun 19) — uses the "Become a Great PM in 30 mins" structural pattern that hit 63K impressions

Most PMs are using AI like a search engine.

—

1/ Stop "asking" the model. Start *handing it the codebase*.

Claude Code can read your repo, your design docs, your past tickets. The moment you stop pasting snippets and start giving it context, the output stops being generic.

2/ Spec like you mean it.

A 200-word PRD gets a 200-word answer. Treat the model like a sharp junior who joined yesterday — assumptions, constraints, what "done" looks like.

3/ Watch it work, don't just collect the output.

Reading the diff as Claude writes is where the PM learning happens. You see what it assumed, where your spec was thin.

4/ Ship the prototype to one user.

You don't need eng to test a hypothesis anymore. A working prototype in front of one real user beats a beautiful Figma every time.
