## 2. The job of social (the core strategic call)

*Recommended call: social is a **top-of-funnel audience + free-course engine**, NOT a direct BWCC sales channel.*

- **~80% of posts** = teaching + authority → CTA to the **free course** (your proven wedge: 2,025 enrolled, 50% open, 26% click — and it feeds the email automations that are *built to convert*).
- **~20% of posts** = direct BWCC, and only on real beats (launch, milestones, scarcity, stunts, close).

The email machine sells. Events sell. Social's job is to keep filling the top of both — and to compound your reputation as the person who actually ships this. Direct-selling on social burns the reach you need; let value do the pulling and let the funnel do the closing.

## 4. Content pillars + ratio

Per week, roughly:

- **50% Teaching** — the save-bait format that already wins. Prompt anatomy, evals, context rot, agent-building, the one-vs-three-Claudes decision. **Every teaching post bridges to the free course.**
- **30% Authority / build-in-public** — show you live this: the agents that write your tweets, the fleet reviewing your PRs, real workflows, behind-the-scenes of this very launch. Feeds the "top builder" play (Epic 4.1).
- **20% Selling BWCC** — rationed, beat-driven only. Launch, seat milestones (N/70), early-bird scarcity, public buyer list, the stunts, the close.

**CTA rule:** teaching/authority → **free course**. Only the 20% sell-beats → **checkout**. Never run two pitch posts back-to-back.
